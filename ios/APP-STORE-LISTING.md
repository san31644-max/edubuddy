## Description

K Education gives Sri Lankan students one engaging place to learn, practise and grow.

Explore grade-appropriate subjects and lessons, review short notes, test your knowledge with quizzes, follow your learning progress and earn trophies as you complete activities. The AI Tutor helps students understand educational topics, while games and calming activities provide purposeful breaks between study sessions.

Features include:

- Structured subjects and textbook-based lessons
- Short notes and guided lesson content
- Educational AI Tutor
- Quizzes, points, trophies and leaderboards
- Personal learning progress and daily activity
- Parent access and student progress visibility
- Educational games and calming activities
- Sinhala, Tamil and English learning support where available
- Secure account and in-app account deletion

Some lessons and features require a K Education Premium subscription. Availability depends on the student's grade and learning medium.

## Review notes

K Education is an educational platform for Sri Lankan students. The app provides structured lessons, short notes, quizzes, progress tracking, trophies, educational games, AI-assisted learning and parent access. It uses a secure WKWebView for frequently updated educational content and adds native iOS navigation, file selection, downloads, sharing, deep links, connection monitoring and an offline recovery screen.

Please use the dedicated review account supplied in App Review Information. It contains fictional demonstration data and allows access to the principal learning flows. The production server must remain online during review.

Premium access: explain the exact purchasing method here before submission. If digital Premium access can be purchased from inside the iOS app, Apple In-App Purchase may be required. Do not submit until this has been reviewed and configured correctly.

The AI Tutor uses an external AI service to answer educational questions. Responses are limited by the student's account and lesson context.

## App Privacy checklist

The bundled privacy manifest says the native wrapper itself does not track users or collect data directly. App Store Connect privacy answers must still describe data collected by the K Education web service, including data entered or generated through the embedded service.

Review the production database and privacy policy before answering. Likely categories include:

- Contact information: name, telephone number and email, if collected
- User content: profile image, questions or uploaded documents, if collected
- Identifiers: account/user ID
- Usage data: lesson openings, quiz activity, progress and app interaction
- Purchases: subscription/payment status, if associated with the account
- Diagnostics: only if server or third-party tools record them

Declare whether each category is linked to the user and its purpose. Do not claim that no data is collected merely because the iOS binary is a wrapper.
